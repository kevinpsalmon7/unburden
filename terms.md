## 1. What Unburden is

Unburden asks you difficult questions and gives you a private page to answer them on. When you have finished a page you decide whether to keep it or burn it. It also writes a short closing page for you, and, once you have kept a few pages, writes new questions from what you wrote.

**Unburden is not therapy.** It is not medical care, not treatment, not a diagnosis, and it does not replace a doctor, a psychologist or any qualified professional. Nothing the app writes back to you is medical advice. If you are in crisis, contact a professional or your local emergency service today.
